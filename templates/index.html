How it works

Dork Scout combines hand-curated Google/Bing dorks with lightweight tech detection inspired by Wappalyzer.

  1. Search engines return candidate domains matching the dork signature
  2. Common noise (amazon, etsy, myshopify.com, etc.) is filtered automatically
  3. We visit the first handful of domains and scan their HTML for known technology signatures
  4. Results are deduplicated and can be exported instantly
