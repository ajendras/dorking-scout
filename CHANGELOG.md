## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added

- Curated Dorks — Hundreds of tested dorks grouped by payment gateways, platforms, and product verticals
- Multi-Engine — Queries Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and generates Yandex links with rate-limit safety
- Niche Explorer — Find stores selling shoes, electronics, books, egifts or any custom vertical
- API-less Scan — Lightweight fingerprinting inspired by Wappalyzer using HTML signatures and headers
- Deduplication — Filters unique domains and removes common noise like marketplaces automatically
- One-click Export — Download discovered domains with metadata as CSV or JSON for further use
- Category Browser — Beautiful cards and filters to browse tech, platform and product categories
- Async Runner — Background workers collect and verify results without blocking the UI
